# Privacy Policy

**Effective October 3, 2026**

Trivia 1-2-3: Pop Culture Game respects your privacy. The app is designed to be played without creating an account or providing personal information.

## Information Collection

Trivia 1-2-3 does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not use:

- Advertising
- Third-party analytics
- Tracking technologies
- Location services
- User accounts
- Social-media integrations

## Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores gameplay information locally on your device, including settings, tutorial progress, game statistics, and purchase status. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

You can remove locally stored information by using **Reset Stats** where applicable or by deleting the app.

## In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment-card information. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.

## Children’s Privacy

Trivia 1-2-3 does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app’s features or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

For questions about this privacy policy, [contact the developer](mailto:dpueppke@hotmail.com).
